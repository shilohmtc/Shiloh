# Client treatment credit — implementation and release boundary

Requested 8 October 2026. Governing roadmap #879 and resume ledger #611 remain the release/status authorities.

Treatment credit is a separate immutable noncash ledger over canonical CRM V2 clients, appointments and booking payment accounts. It does not reuse Rewards unlock/earning rules or gift-voucher semantics. Goodwill requires an amount and reason; service exchange additionally requires a supplier invoice/reference. The reference records evidence of the exchange, not invoice settlement, a paid expense or a tax treatment. No messages are sent.

The smallest safe first version applies credit after completion, only to the same client's treatment balance. Linked accounts require all members completed for that same client; mixed-client or unfinished groups require review. Awaiting deposits, active payment links, unknown/changed prices and other currencies fail closed. Canonical cash receipts/refunds and existing Rewards/welcome credits determine the remaining balance. Treatment value stays unchanged; noncash history appears separately in Reports/CSV and never enters receipt totals, paid expenses or cash-up. Credits have no earning/unlock threshold.

Wallet and payment-account locks prevent concurrent overspending. Operation UUIDs are locked globally and fingerprint the full request including actor and target; exact retries replay, conflicting retries fail. Every committed issuance/use has one immutable entry and atomic CRM audit. FIFO source allocations preserve goodwill/exchange provenance and invoice references. Deferred database constraints reject negative wallets, cross-wallet allocations, overspent sources or incomplete debits.

## Accepted manual correction policy

No automatic expiry. Christel and Reception may correct credit only with explicit `treatment_credit:correct` authority, existing clinic-wide client/scope access, a reason and confirmed review of the original entry and treatment/payment history. Account names/roles alone never authorize a correction.

Reduce unused credit: append a negative `reduce` entry linked to one original issuance, capped by that source's currently unused amount (including amounts explicitly returned to it). The original amount, reason, actor and supplier reference remain immutable. A reduction consumes only that source; it cannot remove credit still applied to treatment.

Return applied credit: append a positive `undo` entry linked to one original application, capped by its not-yet-returned amount. Reverse only its original source allocations, deterministically oldest-source first. In the same wallet/account transaction, return available credit and reduce net noncash credit applied to the recorded treatment account, reopening that amount of its treatment balance. Preserve the original booking/account evidence even if the appointment was later cancelled, refunded, archived or linked to other visits. Active payment links require review before return. No price rewrite, deposit-state update, actual cash receipt/refund or expense is created. Returned credit becomes available under the same source type/reference, not a new gift or a cash balance.

Cancellation/refund never restores credit automatically. Any return is an explicit staff-reviewed action with immutable reason/actor, target and source-allocation history plus an atomic CRM audit. Existing cash refund authority remains capped by actual net cash received; credit cannot fund a cash refund. Reports/CSV show signed corrections, the correction reason, original source reason/reference and corrected entry separately from actual money. Globally locked request IDs, full fingerprints, current authority checks and original-source/return constraints apply to exact retries and concurrent actions. Correction remains possible for archived clients; new issuance/use requires an active client.
