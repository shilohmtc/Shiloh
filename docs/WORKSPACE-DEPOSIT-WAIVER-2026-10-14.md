## Financial and lifecycle preservation

Only the deposit requirement becomes waived and its still-active deposit requests close. Full treatment price, prior money, actual money, credit, outstanding treatment balance, receipt/income history and appointment status remain unchanged. Shared waivers cover the entire existing payment account and all members; approval, active future status, pricing revision, snapshot consistency and existing money are checked under locks. A cancelled/completed/no-show/expired or inconsistent booking is rejected without reinstatement.

Exact replay checks current account, group, complete member set, requirement identity/original amount, pricing revision, current active future eligibility and current authority. Changed input or scope requires reload. Unchanged replay retains the original audit/time without a new write. Audit failure rolls the requirement/request decision back. Verified provider money already inflight on an earlier waived deposit link is recorded once and flagged for review, including after later booking cancellation. It does not become a false paid-deposit flag, automatic refund or invented cancellation policy.

Canonical deposit synchronization never overwrites waived state, including a stale concurrent state projection. Awaiting-deposit queue, client readiness, policy notice, payment and booking confirmation gates read persisted state. Original cancellation/no-show policy trigger remains unchanged and still computes from original member required amounts. A waiver does not invent credit/refund/forfeiture rules.

Deposit-link issuance uses a session financial lock across existing provider/notice work, while its durable notice claims retain their original autocommit semantics. Waiver/provider transactions share that lock. Separate nested policy connections hold no conflicting session-owned row locks. Native PostgreSQL proof exercises the actual policy service on another pooled connection. Cancellation/group/member row locks use fail-closed bounded conflict handling; expiry and bank-payment races retain actual money and never reopen cancelled visits.
